# Intake compliance

ApprovedIntake is centrally authorized per programme and academic year. Institution operators cannot change capacity. A policy version records counted statuses, regular/supernumerary seat treatment, NRI exemption and permitted concurrency. New policy versions preserve prior references. Intake changes retain before/after audit metadata.

capacity_snapshot reads the referenced policy and counts qualifying enrolments. Regular and supernumerary counts remain separate. NRI is an admission attribute, OCI and Foreign National are independent status attributes. OCI/FN alone do not silently consume or exempt seats. Withdrawn/transferred/draft/rejected records are excluded from the default snapshot. Completed admissions can count in the current academic-year snapshot according to policy.

The primary scenario counts 135 regular admissions against 120 approved regular seats and calculates 15 excess. Supernumerary seats do not offset that excess. Snapshot evidence includes policy version and counted statuses. A finding records the count and review action. Reports should be read with their snapshot year and policy reference.
