APAARSetu combines student-level data ingestion, approved-intake monitoring, explainable record review and student-controlled academic disclosure for SIH26263. A Next.js frontend and FastAPI backend maintain separate identities and institutional enrolments in a persistent relational database. Configurable policies distinguish regular seats from authorized supernumerary capacity and independently report NRI, OCI and Foreign National attributes. Transactional imports retain row-level errors, while deterministic checks flag identifier and enrolment inconsistencies for human review. Isolation Forest identifies unusual institution-level numerical patterns with visible features. Ed25519-signed demonstration credentials support selective disclosure, expiry, QR access, revocation and access history. Four portals enforce server-side role and institution boundaries. Five interactive scenarios invoke actual application services. The system demonstrates these workflows using synthetic data and reports measured evaluation results without claiming production accuracy or official APAAR identity authentication.
