APAARSetu addresses SIH26263 through one operational platform for institutional student submissions, APAAR-linked record consistency, enrolment compliance and authorized academic verification. Institutions register admissions individually or preview and map CSV/XLSX files. The backend checks identifier structure, duplicate submissions, demographic inconsistencies, overlapping enrolments and centrally authorized programme intake. Regular and supernumerary capacities remain separate, while NRI category, OCI status and Foreign National status remain independent attributes. Officials inspect evidence, request clarification and record justified decisions. An actual Isolation Forest supplements deterministic rules with institution-level outlier signals. Students control selected-field disclosure of Ed25519-signed demonstration credentials through expiring opaque links and QR references. Consent revocation immediately restricts subsequent access. Four role-specific portals share persistent SQLite data, versioned FastAPI services and audited workflows. Native Windows launch scripts support local operation without Docker or paid AI. The evaluation uses fictional institutions, identities and labelled synthetic hold-out data. Official APAAR and DigiLocker verification are unavailable; no official identity confirmation or government-issued credential is claimed.
